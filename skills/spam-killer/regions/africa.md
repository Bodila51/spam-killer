# Africa: letters and do-not-call, by country of residence

Researched 2026-10-27 on official sources. Re-open the official page before relying on a detail; flows change.

## South Africa

- **Law:** POPIA, Act 4 of 2013 (Government Gazette text on gov.za). In force; enforcement since 1 July 2021.
- **Rights:** s 23(1) confirm whether information is held (free) and get the record, including third parties with access;
  s 24(1) correct or delete information that is inaccurate, excessive, out of date or unlawfully obtained, or that may no
  longer be kept; s 11(3) object to processing, including for direct marketing; s 69(1) direct marketing by SMS, email or
  automated calls needs consent unless you are an existing customer, and each message must include an opt-out (s 69(4)).
- **Deadline:** "within a reasonable time" (s 23) and "as soon as reasonably practicable" (s 24); no fixed number of days.
- **Regulator:** Information Regulator (South Africa). Find its complaint form on its official site at run time.
- **Do-not-call:** the National Opt-Out Registry (National Consumer Commission, under the 2026 CPA regulations) opens to
  consumers from **May 2027**, according to the government's announcement of 7 Oct 2026 (sanews.gov.za). Until then: s 69
  and the company's opt-out.
- **Letter:**
  > To {{COMPANY}}: I, {{NAME}} (phone {{PHONE}}, email {{EMAIL}}), request under POPIA s 23 that you confirm whether you
  > hold my personal information and give me the record, including the third parties with access and where you obtained
  > it. Under s 24(1) I request deletion of my information, and under s 11(3) and s 69 I object to any direct marketing; I
  > have not consented under s 69(1). Please confirm in writing within a reasonable time.
