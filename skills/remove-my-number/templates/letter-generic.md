# Polite request (countries without a region section, or as a base)

Use this when the user's country has no section in `regions/`, or the company is outside any law that covers the user.
It claims **no legal deadline** and cites no law. Write it in the company's language. Show the draft with phone and email
masked; send only after the user's yes, only to a privacy contact found on the company's own website.

---

**Subject:** Request to stop marketing and delete my contact details – {{FULL_NAME}}

Dear {{COMPANY_NAME}} privacy team,

You appear to hold or use my contact details:

- Name: {{FULL_NAME}}
- Phone: {{PHONE}}
- Email: {{EMAIL}}

Please:

1. Stop contacting me for marketing or sales by phone, SMS, email or messenger.
2. Tell me where you obtained my phone number and email address.
3. Delete my contact details, or if you must keep a record so that you do not contact me again, keep only that.
4. Confirm in writing what you have done.

If a data protection law applies to you in my country or yours, please treat this as a request under that law.

Thank you,
{{FULL_NAME}}
