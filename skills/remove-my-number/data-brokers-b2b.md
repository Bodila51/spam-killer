# B2B contact databases and marketing data brokers

These companies sell work and personal contact details (phone, email) to sales teams and marketers.
They are a common source of cold sales calls and emails, worldwide. Same rules as the people-search sites:
own data only, the user's yes before each request, the user does CAPTCHAs, email links and codes, no account creation,
no ID upload, no payment.

## How to search these
Most B2B databases show nothing without an account. Do not create one. Instead:
1. Submit the opt-out / access request with the user's email (after their yes). Many confirm by email whether they hold
   a profile.
2. In EU/UK and other countries with an access right, send an access + source request (Step 5). A reply naming the
   source ("we got your number from X") is the best proof the user can get.
