# Europe: letters and do-not-call, by country of residence

Researched 2026-10-27 on official sources (legislation, regulator and government sites). Before citing an article in a
letter, Claude may re-open the official text; flows and URLs change.

## EU and EEA (GDPR)

Applies to people in the EU/EEA, including non-citizens living there (for example a Ukrainian living in Sofia).

| Right | GDPR article | What the letter asks |
|-------|--------------|----------------------|
| Access, including the **source** of the data | Art. 15(1), source in Art. 15(1)(g) | "Tell me what you hold and where you got it." |
| Stop direct marketing (absolute) | Art. 21(2) and 21(3) | "Stop using my data for marketing." No balancing test. |
| Erasure | Art. 17(1), incl. (c) after an Art. 21(2) objection | "Delete my data." Exceptions exist (legal duties, legal claims). |
| Deadline | Art. 12(3) | One month; extendable by two further months, and the company must tell you within the first month. |

Template: `templates/gdpr-request.md` (all three rights in one letter). Write it in the company's language
(English is fine for international companies). Companies often "suppress" rather than delete so they can honour the
opt-out; that is allowed.

### Do-not-call: what exists per country (checked 2026-10-27)
Registering is the user's step (codes, confirmations), after their yes. None of these stop scam calls: scammers ignore
them. They cut legitimate telemarketing.

| Country | What to do | Official link | Notes |
|---------|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Italy | Registro Pubblico delle Opposizioni | https://www.registrodelleopposizioni.it | Web form or phone. Registering also cancels earlier telemarketing consents. |
| Spain | Lista Robinson (official operator: Adigital) | https://www.listarobinson.es | Web form + email verification; choose channels (calls, SMS, email, post). Up to 2 months. Free. |
| Belgium | Do Not Call Me | https://www.donotcallme.be | Registration by phone only: 02 882 19 75. |
| Ireland | National Directory Database opt-out (ComReg) | https://www.comreg.ie/consumer-information/home-phone/unsolicited-contacts-national-directory-database/ | You tell your phone provider; mobiles are opted out by default. Up to 5 working days. |
| France | Bloctel **ended on 11 Aug 2026**: phone canvassing now needs your prior consent | https://www.economie.gouv.fr/dgccrf/actualites-dgccrf/le-demarchage-telephonique-desormais-interdit-si-vous-ny-avez-pas-consenti | Report unwanted calls on SignalConso: https://signal.conso.gouv.fr |
| Germany | No registry: cold calls to consumers need prior express consent | https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Fachthemen/Telekommunikation/Unternehmenspflichten/Telefonwerbung/start.html | Complain to the Bundesnetzagentur. |
| Poland | No registry: opt-in consent model | — | Withdraw consent from the company; letter (GDPR Art. 21). |
| Bulgaria | No national do-not-call registry found | — | Use GDPR Art. 21 letters; complaints to CPDP (https://www.cpdp.bg). |
| Other EU/EEA | Not covered here | — | Use GDPR Art. 21 letters. Look for an official registry on the telecom regulator's or consumer authority's site; never use a paid "registration service". |

## United Kingdom

- **Law:** UK GDPR + Data Protection Act 2018; marketing calls, texts and emails also fall under PECR.
- **Rights:** same article numbers as the GDPR: Art. 15 (access + source), 17 (erasure), 21(2)–(3) (stop marketing,
  absolute; the ICO says no special wording is needed). Deadline one month (Art. 12(3)), extendable by two months with notice.
- **Regulator:** ICO. Raise it with the company first, then complain:
  https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/data-protection-complaints/data-protection-complaints/ (phone 0303 123 1113).
- **Letter:** `templates/gdpr-request.md`, replacing "Regulation (EU) 2016/679" with "the UK GDPR and the Data Protection
  Act 2018", and adding "I also withdraw any consent to marketing calls, texts or emails under PECR."
- **Do-not-call:** Telephone Preference Service (TPS): https://www.tpsonline.org.uk/register or phone 0345 070 0707. Free.
  Up to 28 days to take effect. Live sales calls only: not texts, recorded calls or scams.

## Switzerland

- **Law:** revised Federal Act on Data Protection (revFADP / nDSG, SR 235.1), in force since 1 Sep 2023.
- **Rights:** Art. 25 access, including available information on the origin of the data; Art. 30(2)(b) processing against
  your express objection violates your privacy unless justified (use it to stop marketing); Art. 32 civil claim for
  correction or deletion.
- **Deadline:** 30 days for access (Art. 25(7)).
- **Regulator:** FDPIC / EDÖB (https://www.edoeb.admin.ch). It takes reports but has no individual complaint procedure for
  private companies; enforcement is through the civil courts.
- **Letter:** use the polite generic letter (`templates/letter-generic.md`) and add: "under Art. 25 revFADP please give me
  access to my data including its origin; I object to any marketing use of my data (Art. 30(2)(b)); please delete my phone
  number and email (Art. 32). Please reply within 30 days."

## Ukraine

- **Law:** Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection" No. 2297-VI.
- **Rights (Art. 8):** know the **source** and purpose of processing and who received the data; access; a reasoned demand to
  object to processing; a reasoned demand to delete unlawfully processed data.
- **Deadlines (per the Commissioner's guidance):** 30 calendar days for an access request; 10 days to consider a reasoned
  demand.
- **Regulator:** Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights:
  https://www.ombudsman.gov.ua/uk/zahist-personalnih-danih ; hotline 1678; e-cabinet https://cabinet.ombudsman.gov.ua/
- **Do-not-call:** no national registry found.
- **Note:** this law protects people in Ukraine and data processed by Ukrainian companies. A Ukrainian living in the EU
  (for example in Bulgaria) uses the GDPR for EU companies, and this law for Ukrainian companies.
- **Letter:** in Ukrainian:
  > {{COMPANY}}. Я, {{NAME}} (телефон {{PHONE}}, e-mail {{EMAIL}}), відповідно до статті 8 Закону України «Про захист
  > персональних даних» прошу повідомити, чи обробляєте ви мої персональні дані, джерело їх отримання, мету обробки та
  > кому їх було передано (30 календарних днів). Також пред'являю вмотивовану вимогу припинити обробку моїх даних для
  > маркетингу та видалити їх. Прошу відповісти на вимогу протягом 10 днів.

## Turkey

- **Law:** KVKK, Law No. 6698 (text on mevzuat.gov.tr).
- **Rights (Art. 11(1)):** (a)–(c) whether data is processed, information about it, purposes; (ç) domestic or foreign
  third parties it was sent to; (e) deletion or destruction; (f) notify those third parties. No express "source" right:
  the letter can still ask.
- **Deadline:** apply to the company in writing first (Art. 13(1)); answer as soon as possible and at the latest within
  **30 days**, free of charge (Art. 13(2)). Then a complaint to the Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of the
  answer, and in any case within 60 days of applying (Art. 14(1)).
- **Do-not-call / commercial messages:** İYS (İleti Yönetim Sistemi), the official consent system: https://iys.org.tr —
  sign in with e-Devlet or the phone number (the user's step) and refuse calls, SMS or email per brand. Providers must
  stop within 3 business days.
- **Letter:** in Turkish:
  > {{COMPANY}}: Ben, {{NAME}} (telefon {{PHONE}}, e-posta {{EMAIL}}), 6698 sayılı KVKK'nın 13. maddesi uyarınca veri
  > sorumlusu olarak size başvuruyorum. 11(1)(a)–(ç) uyarınca kişisel verilerimin işlenip işlenmediğini, amaçlarını ve
  > aktarıldığı yurt içi veya yurt dışı üçüncü kişileri bildirmenizi; 11(1)(e) ve (f) uyarınca verilerimin silinmesini
  > veya yok edilmesini, bu üçüncü kişilere bildirilmesini ve pazarlama amaçlı kullanımına son verilmesini talep ederim.
  > 13(2) uyarınca en geç 30 gün içinde ücretsiz yanıt vermenizi rica ederim.
