# Americas: letters and do-not-call, by country of residence

Researched 2026-10-27 on official sources. Re-open the official page before relying on a detail; flows change.

## United States

### Do-not-call (any US resident)
- **National Do Not Call Registry (FTC):** https://www.donotcall.gov, or call 1-888-382-1222 from the phone you are
  registering. Free; registration does not expire. Sales calls must stop within 31 days. The FTC says scammers ignore it,
  and charities, political calls, surveys and debt collectors are exempt.

### California (CCPA / CPRA)
- **Rights:** delete (Civil Code 1798.105; the business must also tell its service providers); know, including the
  categories of **sources** (right to know); opt out of the sale and sharing of personal information (also by the Global
  Privacy Control browser signal). There is no general right to stop a company's own marketing; the opt-out covers sale
  and sharing.
- **Deadlines:** know/delete/correct: 45 calendar days, extendable by 45 with notice. Opt-out of sale/sharing: as soon as
  feasible, no later than 15 business days. Opt-outs need no identity verification; delete and know requests do.
- **Regulators:** California Attorney General complaint form:
  https://oag.ca.gov/contact/consumer-complaint-against-business-or-company ; California Privacy Protection Agency:
  https://cppa.ca.gov
- **DROP (Delete Request and Opt-out Platform), Delete Act:** one request to all registered data brokers (600+).
  Live: Californians can submit since 1 Jan 2026; brokers process requests from 1 Aug 2026 and check DROP at least every
  45 days; status updates can take up to 90 days. Residency is verified through the California Identity Gateway (the
  user's step). https://privacy.ca.gov/drop/ — offer this **first** to Californians, then the site table for anything left.
- **Letter:**
  > To {{COMPANY}}: I am {{NAME}}, a California resident (phone {{PHONE}}, email {{EMAIL}}). Under the CCPA (Civil Code
  > 1798.105) please delete all personal information you hold about me and direct your service providers to do the same.
  > I opt out of the sale and sharing of my personal information. Under my right to know, please disclose the categories
  > of sources from which you collected it. Please respond within 45 days (opt-out within 15 business days).

### Other US states
About twenty states have comprehensive privacy laws with a right to delete and to opt out of sale and targeted
advertising (for example Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Oregon, New Jersey; Indiana, Kentucky and Rhode Island
from 1 Jan 2026). Rights and deadlines differ by state. At run time, open the user's **state attorney general's** privacy
page, confirm the law's name and deadline there, and adapt the California letter (name the state law, not the CCPA).
If you cannot confirm it, send the generic letter (`templates/letter-generic.md`) without a deadline.

## Canada

- **Law:** PIPEDA (private sector, federal). Quebec residents: also Quebec's private-sector Act (Law 25).
- **Rights:** access (Principle 4.9) including how the data was obtained; withdraw consent to marketing (Principle
  4.3.8); data no longer needed should be destroyed (Principle 4.5). There is no express "right to erasure".
- **Deadline:** 30 days for access, extendable by up to 30 days with notice inside the first 30.
- **Regulator:** Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada: https://www.priv.gc.ca (find "file a complaint" there at
  run time). Quebec: Commission d'accès à l'information: https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca
- **Commercial email:** Canada's anti-spam law (CASL, enforced by the CRTC) requires an unsubscribe in each commercial
  email.
- **Do-not-call:** National DNCL (CRTC): https://lnnte-dncl.gc.ca/en — register home, mobile, fax or VoIP numbers.
  Telemarketers have 31 days. Help line 1-866-580-3625.
- **Letter:**
  > To {{COMPANY}}: I am {{NAME}} (phone {{PHONE}}, email {{EMAIL}}). I withdraw my consent to the use and disclosure of my
  > personal information for marketing (PIPEDA, Principle 4.3.8) and ask you to stop all marketing contact. Under
  > Principle 4.9 please give me access to my personal information and tell me where you obtained it. Please delete it
  > where it is no longer needed (Principle 4.5). I expect a reply within 30 days.

## Argentina

- **Law:** Ley 25.326 (Protección de Datos Personales).
- **Rights:** access (Art. 14, reply within 10 days); rectification and suppression (Art. 16, within 5 business days);
  removal from marketing databases (Art. 27).
- **Regulator:** AAIP. Complaint page:
  https://www.argentina.gob.ar/servicio/denunciar-incumplimientos-de-la-ley-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales
  (attach your original request with proof of receipt; the user's ID is required by the AAIP, which is their step).
- **Do-not-call:** Registro Nacional No Llame (AAIP): https://nollame.aaip.gob.ar — sign in with Mi Argentina (the user's
  step), add lines. Advertisers have up to 30 days.
- **Letter:** in Spanish:
  > Estimados {{COMPANY}}: Yo, {{NAME}}, en virtud de la Ley 25.326 solicito: (1) acceso a mis datos personales,
  > incluidos teléfono {{PHONE}} y correo {{EMAIL}}, y su origen (art. 14); (2) la supresión de esos datos (art. 16);
  > (3) el retiro de mi nombre de sus bases con fines publicitarios (art. 27). Plazos: 10 días para el acceso y 5 días
  > hábiles para la supresión.

## Brazil

- **Law:** LGPD, Lei 13.709/2018 (text on planalto.gov.br).
- **Rights:** Art. 18, I–II confirmation and access; Art. 19, II full declaration including the data's **origin**, within
  15 days; Art. 18, VII who it was shared with; Art. 18, IV and VI deletion (with the retention exceptions of Art. 16);
  Art. 18, IX revoke consent; Art. 18, §2 object to processing based on other grounds when it breaches the law. Requests
  are free (Art. 18, §5). No fixed deadline in the law for deletion.
- **Regulator:** ANPD. Petition portal: https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/canais_atendimento/peticionamento-eletronico-anpd
- **Do-not-call:** Não Me Perturbe (Anatel's official telemarketing block, linked from gov.br/anatel):
  https://www.naomeperturbe.com.br — create a login (name, CPF, email: the user's step), add numbers, choose operators.
  Up to 30 days. Since Sep 2025 all telecom providers must take part. Excludes debt collection and fraud-prevention calls.
- **Letter:** in Portuguese:
  > Eu, {{NAME}} (telefone {{PHONE}}, e-mail {{EMAIL}}), exerço meus direitos previstos na Lei 13.709/2018 (LGPD) perante
  > {{COMPANY}}. Nos termos do art. 18, II e VII, e do art. 19, II, solicito a declaração completa dos meus dados, incluindo
  > a sua origem e com quem foram compartilhados, no prazo de 15 dias. Nos termos do art. 18, IV e VI, solicito a
  > eliminação dos meus dados e, nos termos do art. 18, §2º e IX, oponho-me a qualquer tratamento para marketing e revogo
  > qualquer consentimento. Caso recuse, indique a base legal; reservo-me o direito de peticionar à ANPD.

## Mexico

Mexico replaced its data protection law in March 2025 and dissolved the former regulator (INAI); its functions passed to
the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno. The ARCO rights (access, rectification, cancellation, opposition) remain.
Article numbers and deadlines of the new text could not be confirmed, so: send the generic letter
(`templates/letter-generic.md`) in Spanish, ask for access (including the source), cancellation and opposition to
marketing, and **do not state a deadline**. Do not use old INAI links. PROFECO runs a consumer registry against
advertising calls: find it on profeco.gob.mx at run time and give the link only after it opens on that official domain.
